# Product Positioning Review

Scored 1–5 on six criteria: user clarity, credibility for a hackathon build, differentiation vs Owed / Invoice Chaser, demo-ability in under 3 minutes, PayPal depth, and AI depth.

| Option | Clarity | Credible | Different | Demo | PayPal | AI | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. AI receivables assistant | 5 | 5 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 22 |
| B. Autonomous payment recovery agent | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 22 |
| C. Cash-flow and receivables OS | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 16 |
| D. Agentic AR platform (diagnosis, comms, negotiation, coordination, prediction) | 3 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 20 |
| **E. Two-sided invoice resolution agent:** "every unpaid invoice has a reason; Unstuck finds it, resolves it *with your client*, and settles it in PayPal" | **5** | **5** | **5** | **5** | **5** | **4** | **29** |

## Why E

- A and B put us head-to-head with *Owed* and *Invoice Chaser* ("an AI that chases invoices").
- C and D overclaim for a one-week build and blur the 30-second story.
- E names the thing nobody else in the field does: the **client side**. Collections tools talk *at* clients. Unstuck gives the client a page where they can say what's actually wrong ("we need PO-88412", "I can do $400 now") and get it resolved on the spot, within rules the owner set, as real PayPal state.

## What changes

- **Name and identity:** kept (Unstuck; ledger green and marigold).
- **Tagline:** "Every unpaid invoice has a reason. Unstuck finds it — and resolves it with your client, inside PayPal."
- **Hero order:** (1) money waiting, (2) why it's stuck, (3) what to do next. Answered on the first screen.
- **Explicit contrast in copy and video:** "not a reminder bot."
- **The agent becomes observable:** "Run agent" shows each step it takes (gather, history, diagnose, plan, check rules, act, verify), so the agentic part is visible rather than implied.
