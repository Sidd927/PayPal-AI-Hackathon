## What must be built

1. A project that **meaningfully uses PayPal** developer technology (APIs/SDKs, free sandbox) — integration must be central.
2. A project that **meaningfully uses AI** (model, platform, agent).
3. A **functional prototype/proof of concept** — not a static mockup.

## Judging

**Stage One (pass/fail):** baseline viability, fit with the hackathon theme, meaningful use of PayPal APIs/SDKs.

**Stage Two (scored):** five criteria — Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea (Quality of the Idea), Presentation. The brief states they are equally weighted; search extracts confirm the five criteria but did not surface the weighting text. Rules state judging "may take place in one or more rounds" at the sponsor's discretion. _Confidence: criteria high, weighting medium._

## Prize-combination limit (official rules extract)

> A project can only win up to one (1) Grand Prize and one (1) Sponsor Prize OR one (1) Honorable Mention Prize and one (1) Sponsor Prize.

## Restrictions to respect

- Use of third-party tools/APIs is allowed; sponsor tools are optional (only required for that sponsor's prize).
- Do not use real money — sandbox only.
- No unlicensed music or third-party copyrighted material in the video.
- Submission content must be in English.
- The project must be the entrant's own work created/meaningfully extended during the submission period. This repository was empty on 2026-11-04 and all code was written in-period.
